## Senior Product Manager, Marketplace Growth

Company: Northstar Market Labs  
Location: Remote, Europe-friendly hours  
Employment: Full-time

## Role Overview

Northstar Market Labs is hiring a Senior Product Manager to improve activation, matching quality, and retention across a B2B marketplace. The role will partner with design, engineering, data, and go-to-market teams to identify growth opportunities and ship measurable improvements.

## Responsibilities

- Own the roadmap for seller activation and marketplace quality.
- Use funnel analysis, experiments, and customer research to identify growth opportunities.
- Work with analytics to define success metrics and monitor product health.
- Partner with design and engineering to ship high-impact marketplace features.
- Communicate tradeoffs, roadmap decisions, and launch plans to senior stakeholders.
- Create clear product requirements, experiment plans, and post-launch readouts.

## Requirements

- 5+ years of product management experience.
- Experience with marketplaces, B2B SaaS, or growth products.
- Strong product discovery and user research skills.
- Comfortable using data, SQL, dashboards, and experimentation.
- Experience improving activation, retention, or conversion funnels.
- Clear written communication and stakeholder management.

## Nice To Have

- Experience with supply quality or matching systems.
- Experience working across remote teams.
- Familiarity with marketplace trust, quality, or ranking signals.
